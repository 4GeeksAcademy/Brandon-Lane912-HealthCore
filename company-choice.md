## My AI Agents Idea

### Patient Experience and Access

I am interested in improving HealthCore's outdated websites and making it easier for patients to find the right care. I would start with a mobile-friendly, accessible redesign for both the US and UK sites, with clear clinic information, services, opening hours, costs or payment options, and a straightforward route to booking. The experience should account for each market's locations and processes while feeling like one consistent HealthCore service.

An AI access assistant could answer approved, non-clinical questions about services, locations, and booking, then guide patients to the right appointment or a staff member. It should not give medical advice or collect unnecessary health information. The booking and reminder flows should respect each patient's communication preferences and consent, and measure practical outcomes such as successful bookings, abandoned journeys, and no-shows.

### Compliance and Data Governance

I am also interested in rebuilding how HealthCore oversees compliance and patient data across the US and UK. Before adding more automation, I would establish a clear inventory of systems and data flows, assign owners, and document which jurisdiction's rules apply. A monitoring dashboard could then bring together access and audit events with filters for location, system, user, and patient-record activity, while keeping data access and retention appropriate to each jurisdiction.

An AI compliance assistant could help staff find relevant policies, flag unusual access patterns, and prepare patient data-request packages from the connected systems. It should cite the evidence behind each flag, record its actions, and route decisions such as releasing records or declaring a violation to an authorized person. That keeps the tools useful for early detection and faster reviews without treating an automated score as a legal conclusion.
